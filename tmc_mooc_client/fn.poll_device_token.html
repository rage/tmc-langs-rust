Polls the token endpoint once with the device-code grant (RFC 8628 §3.4).

authorization_pending and slow_down map to DeviceTokenPoll variants; expired_token and access_denied map to MoocClientError::DeviceCodeExpired and MoocClientError::DeviceAccessDenied. Other failures are plain errors.
